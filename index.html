Rupa Anne

I am a Ph.D. researcher at Georgia Tech working at the intersection of machine learning, optimization, and behavioral modeling. I design and evaluate behavioral interventions—badges, leaderboards, and informational messages—to influence travel behavior and optimize mobility outcomes. I combine large-scale network modeling, optimization algorithms (e.g., PSO, bi-level network design), and ML for mobility data to model individual decision-making and drive system-level improvements in efficiency, health, and environmental impact.

My broader interests include reinforcement learning, traffic simulation, decision science, and behavioral economics. I build scalable, data-driven frameworks that bridge human behavior and intelligent systems and validate them with real-world datasets and field experiments.

  • Research areas: network modeling, traffic simulation, decision science, behavioral economics, RL/optimization
  • Labs: ACT Lab & Decision Science & Policy Lab, Georgia Tech
  • Tools: Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Swift/Kotlin, HERE SDK, SUMO

Awards & Activities

Highlights of recognition, research, and outreach.

SpatialConnect 2025 Best Paper Award — ACM SIGSPATIAL Workshop

Our collaborative research, “Joint Optimization of Transit Stops and Routes to Improve Access to Essential Community Destinations,” was awarded Best Paper at the SpatialConnect 2025 Workshop on Spatial Intelligence for Smart and Connected Communities, co-located with ACM SIGSPATIAL.

This work introduces a location-aware optimization framework that jointly determines bus stop placement and route design to maximize access to key destinations—grocery stores, medical facilities, and employment hubs. Applied to the City of Peachtree Corners, Georgia, the model demonstrates how data-driven transit design can strengthen community-scale accessibility and equity in suburban contexts.

This recognition reflects the broader goal of my research: building optimization and decision-support tools that integrate mobility, equity, and community well-being to guide smarter transportation planning.

Transportation Research Board (TRB) Best Paper Award — 2025

Building on foundations in transportation engineering and behavioral modeling, my doctoral research integrates optimization, behavioral interventions, and data analytics to promote sustainable travel. I designed and tested low-cost, mobile app–based interventions blending gamification with health- and environment-focused messaging.

A large-scale randomized trial with more than 10,000 U.S. car owners demonstrated measurable behavior shifts toward walking, biking, and public transit. Beyond individual outcomes, I developed optimization and simulation frameworks that quantify network-level impacts on emissions and public health. For this pioneering work, I received the 2025 TRB Best Paper Award.

Founder — CarbonPlay (Create-X & Sustainable-X)

If measuring carbon is expensive, the people who need it most won’t use it. CarbonPlay was born from that premise — a proof-of-concept that low-cost, verifiable carbon accounting can be achieved through mobile data and transparent validation pipelines. We interviewed 50+ stakeholders, from utilities to city planners, to design a system where trust and traceability are built in, not added later.

Over the course of my PhD, I helped mentor the VIP course at Georgia Tech where I got to mentor undergraduate students to work on real-world live projects. Teaching Experience — Transportion Network Modeling and Analysis & Transportation Systems Analysis

I have teaching experience in two graduate-level courses at Georgia Tech: Transportation Network Modeling & Analysis (TNMA) and Transportation Systems Analysis (TSA). In TNMA, I helped modernize the course by introducing hands-on programming assignments using native Python for network-level optimization and SUMO–TraCI integration for Dynamic Traffic Assignment simulations. These additions transformed the class from a primarily theoretical course into one that emphasizes practical implementation, computational thinking, and real-world network modeling skills.
